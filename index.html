THE THREE-SECOND METHOD

1. Architecture: long nose = front GT; cab-forward = mid-engine; long tail roof = shooting brake; high ride = Purosangue.

2. Hard signature: pop-ups, flat door strakes, triple exhausts, S-duct, rear wing, visor nose, or headlight pattern.

3. Nearest neighbor: resolve the most likely look-alike pair, not the entire lineup.

Look-alike collision course

TEST THE HARD PAIRS
308 vs 328308 = sharper bumpers and more old-school nose. 328 = body-colored bumpers and cleaner integrated face.
348 vs F355348 = strong door strakes, gated tail. F355 = smooth intake and round lights.
360 vs F430360 = rounded oval lights, gentle mouth. F430 = triangular lights, larger air openings.
458 vs 488 vs F8458 = clean flanks/triple exhaust; 488 = scoop behind doors; F8 = 488-derived sides plus deep S-duct.
FF vs GTC4LussoFF = earlier face and plainer sculpting; GTC4Lusso = updated front, lamps and tail.
F12 vs 812 vs 12CilindriF12 = side Aero Bridge, 812 = sharper later design, 12Cilindri = continuous black nose visor.
F40 vs F50F40 = boxed, big flat wing; F50 = organic, wraparound wing and sculpted air ducts.
SF90 vs 296 vs 849SF90 = wide squared mid-engine hybrid, 296 = smoother compact body, 849 = newer visor and angular aero.
